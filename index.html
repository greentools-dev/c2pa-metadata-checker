Content Credentials Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

What are C2PA and Content Credentials?

Content Credentials (created by the C2PA coalition founded by Adobe, Microsoft, Arm, and Intel) act as a digital "nutrition label" for digital media. They record when an image was captured, whether generative AI was used, and what modifications were made.

How does binary inspection detect AI generation?

Leading AI generators (such as Adobe Firefly, DALL-E 3, and Google Imagen) automatically inject JUMBF metadata boxes with digital certificates explicitly identifying the generation model. This tool parses raw binary byte markers directly.

Does stripping C2PA degrade image quality?

No. Our lossless manifest stripper removes only the binary header metadata blocks (APP11 markers in JPEG or JUMB chunks in PNG). The compressed DCT image coefficients and pixels remain bit-for-bit unchanged.

Are my photos uploaded to the cloud?

Never. The parser runs 100% locally in your browser memory using JavaScript DataView and Uint8Array. Confidential images and camera files never touch a server.
